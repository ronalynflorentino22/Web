My Story & Journey

Hello again! My name is Ronalyn S. Florentino. This page is dedicated to sharing a deeper look into who I am, the discipline that drives me, and the goals I am working toward as both a student and an athlete.

🥋 My Athletic Path in Arnis

My journey with Arnis the national martial art and sport of the Philippines started way back in Grade 7. What began as an interest quickly grew into a lifelong passion and commitment. Training and competing over the years has taught me invaluable life lessons: mental toughness, razor-sharp focus, agility, and deep respect for others. Balancing intense physical training from high school straight into college has proven to me that consistency and discipline lead to success.

💻 My Academic Journey in IT

As a 3rd-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student, I immerse myself in learning software systems, coding, and problem-solving. Juggling the logical demands of technology coursework with the physical rigor of martial arts training has sharpened my time-management skills and taught me how to stay calm under pressure.

🎯 Core Values & Vision

Whether I am holding an arnis stick on the mat or writing code on my screen, my mindset remains the same: give your absolute best and never stop improving. I take great pride in breaking boundaries as both a technologist and an athlete, and I am excited for what the future holds.
